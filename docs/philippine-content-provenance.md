## Per-item provenance record

Every milestone, learning activity, story/rhyme, or caregiver-facing developmental statement must have a record with these fields before it is marked reviewed:

- **Item ID and title**
- **Item type:** observation prompt, activity, story/rhyme, or caregiver guidance
- **Intended app age band:** product navigation category only
- **Source title and issuing organization**
- **Stable source URL**
- **Edition/version and publication date:** use “not stated” if the source does not provide one
- **Exact locator:** page, section, competency code, table, or paragraph; include a short supporting quotation only when necessary and permitted
- **Relationship to source:** direct link, paraphrase/adaptation, or original activity informed by source
- **Adaptation notes:** explain how wording/activity was adapted and why it remains faithful to the source
- **Language and localization notes**
- **Safety/accessibility considerations**
- **Reviewer name/role and review date**
- **Review status:** draft, source-checked, subject-matter-reviewed, approved, or retired
- **Known limitations and follow-up**

Do not use a source's general landing page as sufficient evidence for an individual content claim when the relevant document and locator can be identified.

## Review rules

1. Keep product age bands separate from official curriculum stages and any clinical/assessment age ranges.
2. Use ECCD Council materials for relevant Philippine early-childhood context and DepEd curriculum materials for school-readiness/Kindergarten learning competencies where applicable; do not assume these source types are interchangeable.
3. Do not present app content as an official ECCD Council or DepEd checklist, validated screening instrument, diagnosis, or clinical recommendation.
4. A citation alone does not make an item valid, safe, developmentally appropriate, or licensed for reproduction. Check content permissions and attribution requirements before copying or adapting text, images, stories, songs, or activity sheets.
5. Have an appropriate early-childhood subject-matter adviser review content before it is labeled “reviewed” or “approved.” Record the reviewer and date.
6. Use neutral, non-judgmental language. A caregiver observation or a single unobserved behavior must not be framed as proof of delay or a developmental condition.
7. Do not populate a production content catalog with unsourced claims. Draft content may be developed in a clearly labeled working area while source and reviewer fields remain incomplete.

## Initial source disposition

- **ECCD Checklist materials:** candidate source only. The existence of a checklist and scoring materials is confirmed on the ECCD Council resources page; the precise edition, intended user qualifications, administration rules, licensing, and suitability for any LittleSteps use still require document-level review. Do not reproduce checklist items or scoring.
- **National Early Learning Framework / National Early Learning Curriculum:** candidate framework/curriculum sources linked from the ECCD Council resources page. Retrieve and review the exact documents before mapping any item to them.
- **DepEd Kindergarten Curriculum Guide:** candidate educational curriculum source, including the current MATATAG guide linked by DepEd. Verify the exact version and relevant competencies before adapting learning activities.
- **Republic Act No. 10410:** policy context only; not a source for individual developmental milestone items.
- **CDC milestone checklists:** may be considered as a separate educational reference only after reviewing their stated purpose and limitations. They must not be represented as a validated Philippine screening instrument.

This issue can be closed only when the team has recorded the exact source versions and usage/attribution constraints, agreed on the item-level record, and assigned subject-matter review. Until then, no item should be labeled authoritative, clinically validated, or reviewed.
