# 2. Executive Summary

**Plan section:** 2  
**Version:** 1.0 draft, 23 October 2026  
**Status:** Draft, written after Sections 3–16. Keep it to one page in the PDF; update it if any figure in those sections changes.  
**Related Documents:** [Business Case](03-business-case.md), [Project Charter](04-project-charter.md), [Schedule](08-schedule.md), [Cost Estimate and Budget](09-cost-estimate-and-budget.md)

---

**The problem.** Parents of young children in Iloilo City's La Paz and Lapuz districts get learning-at-home ideas from their Child Development Center through printed handouts, notebooks and group chats. Home records get lost, parents run out of low-cost activity ideas, and the popular baby apps are foreign, rarely in Filipino or Hiligaynon, and often show diagnostic-style scores. In our estimates, each center loses about 8 children's home records a year, and each Child Development Worker (CDW) spends about 80 hours a school year preparing handouts.

**The proposed system.** LittleSteps is a free Flutter app (one codebase for Android, iOS and the web, piloted on Android) for parents and other caregivers of children aged 0–5. They can keep a private record of what they notice, plan short, screen-free activities from a catalog of 80 reviewed Philippine activities, stories and rhymes, see a factual weekly summary, and ask an AI helper for ideas that match the time and materials they have. It never scores or labels a child, and journal notes never leave the parent's account.

**The cost.** **₱1,083,185**, including a 10% contingency reserve of ₱98,471, estimated bottom-up from 49 work packages. Running costs after handover are about ₱5,000 a month.

**The duration.** Eight months, **4 January to 27 August 2027** (166 working days), including an eight-week pilot at two Child Development Centers in La Paz from 3 June to 28 July 2027. The critical path runs through the backend and app development, testing, the pilot and the handover.

**What the organization gains.** Parents keep their child's home record and get ideas that use what they already have. CDWs spend less time on handouts: about 2,960 hours and ₱166,500 in printing a year across the district's estimated 37 centers. The office gets a tested, privacy-safe tool that supports the city's duties under the ECCD System Act of 2025 and can be extended to other districts. Success is measured by eight criteria in the charter, including 30 pilot households, weekly use by 60% of them, a usability score of at least 70 and approval by the Data Protection Officer.

**What we ask.** We ask the OIC District Head of the CSWDO La Paz–Lapuz District office to approve the charter, the budget and the schedule, to commit CDWs' time for the pilot, and to ask the city's IT office to name a maintainer by May 2027.

*The client was chosen from public information and was not contacted for this course plan; per-center figures are the team's estimates (Appendix A).*
