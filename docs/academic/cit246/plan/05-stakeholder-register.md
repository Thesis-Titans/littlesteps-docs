## 5.2 Power/Interest Grid

High influence means rated High in §5.1; lower influence means Medium or Low. Higher interest means High or Medium; low interest means Low.

| | Low interest | Higher interest |
|---|---|---|
| **High influence** | **Keep satisfied:** Punong Barangay and Barangay Council (3) | **Manage closely:** OIC District Head (1), CSWDO city head office (2), Data Protection Officer (6), city ECCD office (13) |
| **Lower influence** | **Monitor:** DILG city field office (14) | **Keep informed:** CDWs (4), parents (5), content reviewer (7), Barangay Health Workers (8), senior CDW (9), contractors (10), project team (11), CIT 246 faculty (12) |

Within "keep informed", the CDWs (4), parents (5) and the senior CDW (9) are also involved directly, through interviews, co-review of content and onboarding sessions, because pilot adoption depends on them.

## 5.3 Managing the Stakeholder Who Prefers the Project Did Not Happen

The senior CDW (stakeholder 9) has run home-learning support on paper for years and sees the app as extra work and a way to judge CDWs by usage numbers. Because families listen to them, their quiet resistance could stall the pilot more than any technical problem. The project team will meet them in the first two weeks, before the charter review, to hear their concerns directly and ask them to co-review the activity catalog, so their experience shapes the content. The plan will state in writing that app usage is never used to evaluate CDWs and that the app adds no reporting duty. Their paper process stays available for families without smartphones, so the app is an option, not a replacement. The sponsor will invite them to co-lead one caregiver onboarding session, and the project manager will check their view at each two-week demo and raise unresolved concerns with the sponsor rather than work around them.
