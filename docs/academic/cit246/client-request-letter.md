**Request for a courtesy meeting and short interviews for a student project on home learning for young children**

Greetings from the College of Information and Communications Technology of West Visayas State University.

We are BS Information Technology students working on a coordinated project for three courses: CIT 246 IT Project Management, CC 207B Applications Development and Emerging Technologies, and CIT 210 Web Systems and Technologies. Our project, **LittleSteps**, is a free mobile app that helps parents of children aged 0 to 5 continue learning at home. It suggests short, screen-free activities that use ordinary household materials, lets parents keep a private record of what they notice, and gives no diagnostic scores or pass/fail labels.

In CIT 246 we are writing a project management plan for how such an app could be built and piloted for the Child Development Centers your office supervises. To make the plan realistic, we respectfully ask for:

1. **A 30-minute courtesy meeting with you**, to understand how the centers in La Paz and Lapuz support home learning today, and whether your office would be willing to act as the plan's sponsor for this academic exercise.
2. **A 30-minute interview with one or two Child Development Workers**, about how home-learning activities and observations are shared with families today.
3. **A 20-minute session with three to five parents** who agree to take part, to try a clickable design of the app on a phone and tell us what is clear or confusing.

We would like to schedule these between **[date]** and **[date]**, at a time and place convenient for your office.

**How we will handle information.** Taking part is voluntary, and anyone may stop at any time. We will not collect children's names, photos or records. We will record only general answers and counts, without names, unless a person agrees in writing to be named. The app design uses sample data only. Our notes will be used only for our coursework and will be shared with you on request.

This is an academic exercise. It does not commit your office to funding, adopting or endorsing the app.

Thank you for considering our request. You may reach us through the contact details below.
