## 1. The Organization

The client organization for this project is the **Barangay Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) Center / Community Child Development Network** (under local government unit oversight in Iloilo City). 

The center coordinates early childhood programs, tracks developmental progress for children aged 0–5 years (0–71 months), and provides early-learning resources to parents and home caregivers. Currently, child development workers (CDWs) and families rely on printed paper checklists, physical notebooks, and ad-hoc chat groups to track milestones and communicate home learning activities.

---

## 2. The Problem

Early childhood development during the foundational 0–5 age window faces three interrelated challenges:

1. **Fragmented and Vulnerable Observation Records:** Caregivers record observations on loose paper or handwritten notebooks that are frequently misplaced, damaged, or inaccessible when consulting pediatricians or child development workers. There is no cumulative, longitudinal history of child engagement.
2. **Scarcity of Screen-Free, Low-Cost Home Learning Activities:** Caregivers frequently lack structured, age-appropriate offline activity ideas that make use of ordinary household materials, leading to parental frustration and excessive reliance on passive smartphone screen time.
3. **Data Privacy Risks and Inappropriate Diagnostic Scoring:** Commercial mobile apps often monetize sensitive child data or generate unvalidated, pseudo-clinical "diagnostic scores" and pass/fail labels that trigger parental anxiety without clinical validity.

---

## 3. The Proposed System

**LittleSteps** is a coordinated academic software system designed to make early-learning support and observational tracking accessible, secure, and manageable for caregivers and child development practitioners. 

The system provides a **Flutter mobile application** (developed for CC 207B) and a **React web application** (developed for CIT 210) powered by a shared backend API and database. LittleSteps implements six distinct core features:

1. **Child Profile and Milestone Tracking:** Caregiver-managed child records with observational prompts aligned with early-learning standards, explicitly avoiding clinical pass/fail scoring.
2. **Daily Activity Planning and Tracking:** Interactive scheduling and tracking of everyday learning routines using common household materials.
3. **Private Observation Journal:** Caregiver-controlled qualitative journaling with strict encryption and isolation from external AI processing.
4. **Story, Rhyme, and Learning Activity Library:** Curated, age-banded collection of culturally resonant stories, rhymes, and offline learning activities.
5. **Weekly Progress Summary:** Objective recaps of completed activities and noticed milestones without making unsupported developmental claims.
6. **Constrained AI Activity Suggestion Helper:** Direct user-facing recommendation helper that matches activities to caregiver time, language, and available household items from an approved catalog, backed by deterministic offline fallbacks and strict compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
